===============================================================================
PRODUCT BOUNDARY (decided 2026-07-13, re-affirmed 2026-07-14)
===============================================================================

NearU is discovery and coordination only.

Do not build a wallet, checkout, payout flow, connected account flow, card
processor, payment webhook, escrow, platform fee, top-up, send-funds flow, or any
feature that requires users to pay through NearU.

Build verticals as:
- listings/posts
- browse/search
- profiles and trust
- reports/blocks
- reviews
- messages and realtime coordination
- notifications

The live `profiles` table still has dormant legacy columns named
`wallet_balance`, `stripe_account_id`, and `stripe_customer_id`. They exist only
because they are already in the database schema. Do not select them, display them,
write them, or build from them.

Marketplace `listings.price_cents` is a displayed listing field only. It is not a
NearU transaction, collection, verification, or enforcement mechanism.
